## 5. Planning Points to Discuss

*On a real client: allowance figures from your technical source or the adviser, cashflow output from your modelling tool (Voyant, CashCalc, Truth or Timeline). This section lists points for discussion — it never recommends.*

- **Crystallisation timing and tax-free cash (Priya):** Priya's email asks
  about taking the tax-free lump sum and leaving the rest. To discuss: full
  versus phased crystallisation, what the lump sum is for, and that taking
  only tax-free cash does not trigger the money purchase annual allowance —
  whereas taking taxable income from flexi-access drawdown would. She is
  still contributing through her consultancy, so that matters.
- **Sustainability of planned withdrawals (Priya):** her stated target is
  £18,000 a year gross from the SIPP after tax-free cash. Against a
  crystallised fund of about £309,000 that is roughly 5.8% a year — to
  discuss against the May 2026 cashflow model, her capacity for loss (last
  recorded before she reduced her hours) and the gap years before her State
  Pension starts. Run `/retirement-income-review` before the meeting.
- **Carry forward (Tom):** Tom expects a bonus in March 2027. To discuss
  whether additional contributions make sense and whether he has unused
  annual allowance from the three previous tax years. Carry-forward
  headroom: — pending pension input amounts from Tom's scheme.
- **State Pension (Tom):** forecast still outstanding; needed before any
  household income plan can be finalised.
- **GIA and ISA use:** approx. £11,000 unrealised gain in the joint GIA.
  To discuss: use of this year's CGT annual exempt amounts and ISA
  subscriptions, and the 30-day share-matching rule if any holding is sold
  and bought back.
- **Expressions of wish:** Priya's SIPP nomination is dated 2015 and Tom's
  workplace DC expression of wish 2014 — both before the grandchildren.
  To discuss whether they still reflect the clients' intentions. Run
  `/death-benefits-and-tax-brief` for the full position.
- **Pensions and inheritance tax from April 2027:** from 6 April 2027 most
  unused pension funds and death benefits are expected to come into the
  estate for inheritance tax. Awareness point only — to discuss whether
  the Hartleys want to review their wider estate plans with their
  solicitor. Check final legislation and HMRC guidance before relying on
  detail.

## 8. Talking Points & Anticipated Questions

**Talking points:**
1. The household plan still funds the core spending you set — the question
   this year is how and when Priya starts drawing, not whether she can.
2. The cash held in Priya's SIPP has held back returns slightly; that was
   deliberate ahead of crystallisation, and it is a choice to revisit.

**Anticipated questions:**
- Expect: "Can I take the lump sum now and leave the rest invested?" —
  cover the options and their consequences, including the MPAA point; the
  decision and any advice are yours.
- Expect: "Is £18,000 a year safe?" — walk through the cashflow model and
  the assumptions behind it; don't give a yes or no from this pack.
- Expect: "What does the 2027 change mean for our children?" — awareness
  only; point to the solicitor for wider estate planning.
