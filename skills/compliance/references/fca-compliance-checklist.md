# FCA Compliance Checklist for Client-Facing Content
*Reference for the Pensions Adviser (UK) compliance skill. A snapshot drafted September 2026: verify against the current FCA Handbook (COBS 4, PRIN 2A, COBS 19, SYSC 9) and FCA guidance on the FCA website (FG24/1 on social media, the Consumer Duty materials, retirement income and pension transfer publications) for high-stakes reviews. It is not kept current automatically.*

## 1. Scope Determination

- [ ] **Firm status.** Directly authorised (DA), or an appointed representative (AR) of a principal or network? An AR cannot sign off its own financial promotions: the principal or network must approve them, so the sign-off route goes to the network's compliance team.
- [ ] **Advice status.** Does the firm hold itself out as independent or restricted? Any description of the service must match.
- [ ] **Is the communication a financial promotion?** An invitation or inducement to engage in investment activity, communicated in the course of business (section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000). Indicators:
  - It is one-to-many (newsletter, social post, website page, seminar deck, mailshot, video) and markets the firm's services, a product, an investment approach or a course of action such as consolidating or transferring pensions, OR
  - It is addressed to one person but goes beyond their personal advice and invites them to take up a product or service (a personalised promotional letter, a prospect follow-up selling the service), OR
  - It contains a testimonial, review, rating or award used to promote the firm.
- [ ] **Usually not a financial promotion on its own:** a personal communication about the client's own existing advice (a suitability-related letter, a review summary, a reply to the client's own question), scheduling and administrative messages, and purely factual information that invites no action. These still carry sections 2 and 5 in full.
- [ ] **Audience.** Retail or professional client? One individual, or one-to-many? The retail communication rules apply to retail audiences. Assume retail unless told otherwise.
- [ ] **Pensions topics with specific rules** (section 4): defined benefit (DB) or other safeguarded-benefit transfers, drawdown and retirement income, pension consolidation, tax-free cash, pension scams and unregulated or high-risk investments, Pension Wise guidance.
- [ ] **Whatever the answer above:** the fair, clear and not misleading rule (COBS 4.2.1R) and the Consumer Duty (PRIN 2A, including the consumer understanding outcome) apply to **all** client communications, promotion or not.

## 2. Core Standards (all client communications)

Flag any content that:
1. [ ] Is not **fair, clear and not misleading** (COBS 4.2.1R), including by what it leaves out
2. [ ] Emphasises **potential benefits** without a **fair and prominent indication of relevant risks**
3. [ ] **Disguises, diminishes or obscures** important items, statements or warnings (small print, buried footnotes, a warning pushed below the fold)
4. [ ] Would not be **understood by the average member of the group it is directed at**, or is likely to be received by
5. [ ] Makes a **material claim of fact** the firm could not substantiate if asked (charges, outcomes, comparisons, "most clients")
6. [ ] Falls short of the **Consumer Duty consumer understanding outcome**: information that is not tailored to the audience, not timely, not tested where testing is appropriate, or that leaves the customer unable to make an informed decision
7. [ ] Causes **foreseeable harm** or relies on **sludge or pressure**: undue urgency, friction to exit or cancel, framing that exploits behavioural biases, discouraging customers from seeking guidance

## 3. Performance and Projections

**Past performance** (COBS 4.6):
- [ ] Not the **most prominent feature** of the communication
- [ ] Covers the preceding **five complete 12-month periods** (or the whole period since inception if shorter), each based on complete 12-month periods
- [ ] Periods end as recently as practicable (check the firm's policy on the reporting date used)
- [ ] **Reference period and source** stated
- [ ] Carries the warning that **past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results**
- [ ] **Currency warning** where figures are in a currency other than sterling (returns may rise or fall with exchange rates)
- [ ] **Gross vs net** clear: where figures are gross, the effect of commissions, fees and charges is disclosed
- [ ] Not cherry-picked: no favourable periods, funds or clients shown without fair and balanced context

**Simulated past performance** (COBS 4.6.6R): only where it relates to an instrument or index, is based on actual past performance of something the same or underlying, meets the past-performance conditions above, and carries the "not a reliable indicator" warning.

**Future performance and projections** (COBS 4.6.7R–4.6.8R):
- [ ] Not based on, and does not refer to, simulated past performance
- [ ] Based on **reasonable assumptions supported by objective data**
- [ ] Gross figures accompanied by the effect of charges
- [ ] Carries a prominent warning that **forecasts are not a reliable indicator of future performance**
- [ ] **Cashflow-model outputs** (Voyant, CashCalc, Truth, Timeline and similar) presented as illustrations on stated assumptions, never as promises of what the client will have or be able to spend

**Any past, simulated or future performance escalates to the firm's compliance officer on its own, whatever severity it is rated.**

## 4. Pensions-Specific Rules

- [ ] **DB and safeguarded-benefit transfers.** Content must not promote transferring. The FCA's starting assumption is that a DB transfer is unsuitable (COBS 19.1.6G). No "free" transfer-value analysis framed as an inducement; no contingent-charging language (contingent charging banned from 1 October 2020, with narrow carve-outs); the loss of guaranteed, typically inflation-linked, lifetime benefits must be stated. Members with safeguarded benefits worth more than £30,000 must take appropriate independent advice before transferring or converting. **Any DB or safeguarded-benefit transfer content escalates on its own.**
- [ ] **Drawdown and retirement income.** Balanced risk: investment risk, the risk of running out of money, sustainability of the withdrawal rate, and the fact that income is not guaranteed. No implied "safe" withdrawal rate.
- [ ] **Tax-free cash.** Framed without urging early or full access; mentions that taking it reduces the fund for later income and that tax rules may change. Content that uses a fiscal event as urgency ("take it before the Budget") is a pressure tactic.
- [ ] **Pension consolidation.** Claims such as "lower fees" or "better performance" need substantiation and must be balanced by possible loss of guarantees or valuable benefits (guaranteed annuity rates, protected tax-free cash, protected pension age), exit charges, and loss of employer contributions.
- [ ] **Pension Wise / MoneyHelper.** Where content concerns accessing pension savings, reference to free, impartial Pension Wise guidance (for people aged 50 and over) is expected.
- [ ] **High-risk and unregulated investments.** No promotion of restricted mass market investments or non-mass market investments to retail pension clients outside the FCA's rules for them (categorisation, appropriateness, prescribed risk warnings); no promotion of unregulated investments via pensions.
- [ ] **Scam risk.** Content touching transfers, "pension liberation", early access before minimum pension age, cold-call offers or overseas schemes should carry scam-awareness language (FCA ScamSmart) rather than anything that looks like one.

## 5. Testimonials, Reviews, Ratings and Social Media

- [ ] **Testimonials and reviews** are permitted but must be **genuine, representative and not misleading**: no selective display that hides unfavourable reviews, no reviews presented as typical when they are not.
- [ ] Any **incentive** given for a review (payment, discount, prize draw) is disclosed. Fake or incentivised-but-undisclosed reviews also fall under the consumer protection rules enforced by the CMA (Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024).
- [ ] **Ratings and awards:** provider, date, basis of the rating and whether anything was paid to obtain or use it.
- [ ] **Social media (FG24/1):** each post must be standalone compliant; risk warnings prominent and not hidden behind "see more" or a link; character limits are no excuse for missing balance; finfluencer or affiliate arrangements mean the firm is responsible for the promotion.
- [ ] **Any testimonial or review escalates on its own.**

## 6. Language Red Flags (any audience, any channel)

Flag these words and patterns for rework:
- "Guaranteed", "assured", "promise", "no risk", "risk-free", "can't lose", "safe" (as an absolute)
- "Will" + outcome ("will grow", "will last", "will outperform"): prefer "aims to", "is designed to", "has historically"
- "Best", "top", "number one", "leading", "cheapest" without substantiation and source
- "FCA approved" or anything implying FCA authorisation is an endorsement of the content or the product
- Urgency and pressure ("act now before the Budget", "limited window", "don't miss out")
- Incomplete fee or charge statements ("we only charge X" when platform and fund charges also apply; ongoing advice charges not stated clearly). Charges link to the Consumer Duty price and value outcome.
- Tax statements as certainties ("this is tax-free", "no inheritance tax") without "depends on individual circumstances and may change"
- Vague or euphemistic references to risk ("some ups and downs") that understate it

## 7. Disclosure Library

**Adapt to your firm's approved wording.** Every item below is a starting point, not approved text:

- **Status (directly authorised):** "[Firm name] is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. Financial Services Register number [FRN]."
- **Status (appointed representative):** "[Firm name] is an appointed representative of [Principal name], which is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. Financial Services Register number [FRN]."
- **Advice status:** "We offer [independent / restricted] advice. [For restricted: This means we (describe the restriction).]"
- **Capital at risk:** "The value of investments and any income from them can fall as well as rise and you may get back less than you invested."
- **Past performance:** "Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Figures are shown [net / gross] of [charges] for the period [dates]. Source: [source]."
- **Forecasts:** "Forecasts are not a reliable indicator of future performance. Projections are illustrations based on the assumptions stated and are not guaranteed."
- **Currency:** "Returns may increase or decrease as a result of currency fluctuations."
- **Pensions access:** "You cannot normally access your pension savings until age 55 (rising to 57 from 6 April 2028), unless you have a protected pension age."
- **Tax:** "Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may be subject to change in the future."
- **DB transfer:** "Transferring out of a defined benefit pension scheme means giving up guaranteed, lifelong income that typically rises with inflation. For most people, keeping these benefits is likely to be in their best interests."
- **Drawdown:** "Income from drawdown is not guaranteed. Withdrawals, charges and falls in the value of your investments can reduce your fund, and you could run out of money."
- **Pension Wise:** "Pension Wise from MoneyHelper offers free, impartial guidance on your pension options if you are 50 or over. [Add the current MoneyHelper link and phone number.]"
- **Not FCA-regulated services:** "The Financial Conduct Authority does not regulate [some forms of tax advice / estate planning / will writing / trusts]."
- **Testimonial or review:** "This review was provided by a [current client / former client / non-client]. [No incentive was / An incentive of (describe) was] given for it. Individual experiences may vary."
- **Rating or award:** "[Rating/award] awarded [date] by [provider] based on [basis]. [Nothing was / (describe) was] paid to obtain or use it."

## 8. Record Keeping

- [ ] Keep records of **financial promotions** (COBS 4.11) and **client communications** (SYSC 9) in line with the firm's own record-keeping policy; retention periods vary by business type.
- [ ] **Pension transfer, conversion and opt-out material is kept indefinitely.**
- [ ] For MiFID business, relevant **telephone and electronic communications must be recorded** and kept (COBS 11.8).
- [ ] Keep the substantiation behind material claims of fact, the evidence behind reviews and ratings, and the approval record (including the principal's or network's approval for an AR).
- [ ] **Off-channel communications:** business communications on personal WhatsApp, text or personal email that the firm does not capture breach recording and record-keeping expectations, and the FCA has taken action over this. All business communications go through approved, recorded channels.

## 9. Verdict Rubric

- **Fail**: breaches a rule or a core standard as written; must be fixed before sending
- **Flag**: likely fine with a risk warning, qualifier, substantiation or minor rewording; show the fix
- **Note**: acceptable, but a best-practice improvement is available
